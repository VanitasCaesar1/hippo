# Brain: links that read, an overview that fills the view, a drag that feels physical

Status: accepted 21 Nov 2026, to build in 0.8.3; amended the same day after the third, fourth and fifth reviews (docked panel, links out and in on trunks of their own, badges on the root of links out, hovered lines in lanes of their own, edge chips for lines out of view, a drop never rescales). Amends [brain-arrange.md](brain-arrange.md) and [app-night-map.md](app-night-map.md) › Brain.

## Background

0.8.2 drew every link at every zoom. On a 300-note map with 1,000 links that was a mesh under the cards, and links that had to cross a column of cards fell into a band of detours below the map. The entry note, linked from most notes, fanned out at its edge. Zoomed out, a 300-note map was a strip across the top of the window. A dragged block was an empty outline, and the zoom bar and the Ask bar floated over the map.

## Rules

### Lines

- **No line at rest.** Brain draws the lines of one card: the one under the pointer, else the selected note (at Out, the card of its folder). Moving the pointer from a card onto one of its lines keeps that card in focus.
- **Out and in, apart.** A card's links run on up to four trunks: links out to the right (which also serves cards in its own column), links in from the right, and the same two to the left. Each trunk leaves the card at a port of its own (a card's own trunks leave around its middle, 30 % of its height apart), drops a branch in the gap beside every column it reaches, and the branch splits into short stubs, one per card. Lines that run together share their points exactly, so they draw as one line.
- **One head per line, where its links arrive.** A line of links out ends in an arrowhead at the card it reaches; a line of links in ends in one at the card in focus (the hovered or selected card); a line's start is bare. The same at every zoom, at rest, under hover and faint during a drag, in both renderers. Lines into one card edge meet it at one anchor (a left edge a little above its middle, a right edge a little below), so two heads never stack.
- **One badge per trunk of links out, on its root.** It counts the trunk's links (`13`) on the stretch from the card to the trunk's first turn, which every line of that trunk runs along, so hovering the badge or the root beside it gives one number, and the same card shows the same count at Out, Mid and In. A trunk of links in carries none, and no line in ever touches a badge: hippo records the card's other lines near each root, and a badge is not drawn at a zoom where it would touch one, or where the root is too short to hold it its half-width plus 4 px from the card. A badge stays 8 px inside the canvas, on a halo of ground that breaks any guide behind it.
- **Lanes.** In each gap a trunk has a fixed slot, counted from the gap's middle toward its card: the selected card's links out 11 points toward the card (8 at Mid and In), its links in 11 points past the middle; a hovered card's in the outer slots, 33 points either side (24 at Mid and In). So a hovered card's lines never share a vertical with the selection's, and a trunk of links in never crosses a badge's root. Gaps between columns are 128 points.
- **Hover.** While another card is hovered, the selection's lines step back (30 % over the ground), lose their badges and chips, and drop any line to the hovered card or along a hovered line (`map::beside`); the hovered card's lines are drawn over them in lime, routed as hovered (`Layout.hovered`: their trunks leave a little lower, 14 points at Out and 8 at Mid and In, and their channels sit to one side), so a hovered card's links never ride another card's trunk.
- **The pointer on a line.** The lines under it turn lime, drawn last, and their heads light where their links arrive; the status says `START HERE → Style guide, Decisions · 2 links` for links out and `START HERE ← On-call · 1 link` for links in (up to three names, else `4 others`), with Lucide's arrows. `Layout::lines_at` answers both a line and a badge.
- **Lines to cards out of view.** A line runs on to the canvas edge, and where it leaves the view it ends in a small chip on the border, 8 px inside: the card's name (and `+2` for more leaving there), the links, and an arrow out (`Folder 7 · 3 →`). Lines leaving within 28 px of each other share a chip. A click on a chip pans to its card, keeping the zoom. No chips show while a block is lifted or blocks slide. Trunks never run past their furthest branch.
- **Heads never meet across a gutter**, and **straight where it can be**: a line alone at both its ends between neighbouring columns runs straight when its ends are within 16 points, a target within 16 points of the trunk's height where it branches is met level (no stair-step), and points in the middle of a straight run are dropped.
- **Clear of cards and of each other.** Lines turn only in the gaps between columns. A trunk keeps its height across a column while it stays 20 points (Out) or 12 points (Mid and In) from that column's cards, folder name bands and other trunks; else, in the gap before the column, it turns to the middle of the nearest free channel at least twice that high, or 1.8 times the clearance below everything. Frames at Mid and In are 48 points apart, so a channel between two runs down its middle and never along a frame's border.
- **Drawn quietly**, from `hippo_view::night::map::STYLE`, which both Hippo.app and the web demo read: one weight, 1.5 px, at every zoom; corners round at 7 px; arrowheads 7 px. The selected note's lines are a quiet neutral (the text colour at 55 % over the ground, one opaque colour so lines that run together never draw brighter); a hovered card's, and any line under the pointer, are the hover stroke: lime `#C8F031` on dark, a darker lime `#5E8C00` on light (4:1 on white, yellower than the fixed mark's green). Nothing is drawn over the map but heads, badges and edge chips.

### Cards

- One resting edge for every card. The selection ring stays while another card is hovered; hover adds an edge in the hover stroke and a soft lift, at every level.
- One size per level for every card's words: at Out names at 40 points on up to two lines, counts at 26, note rows at 28; at Mid titles at 16, summaries at 14, folder names at 14.5 and their counts at 13 just after the name. A word that does not fit is cut with `…`, never set smaller. Widths are estimated per capital, so `START HERE` takes one line.
- At Out a folder card shows a folder icon, a pinned note its flag (the entry note) or note icon. A folder card's note count heads its legend; its fixed, waiting, rejected and stale counts sit in two columns below.
- On a map of more than 8 blocks a folder card shows its name and its counts only, each kind of count (fixed, waiting, rejected, stale) in one place on every card; a zero is left out and its place kept empty. Nothing is drawn under 11 px on screen: below `11 / 28` the note rows go, below `11 / 26` the counts; at Mid a note card's summary and chips keep 11 px when their size would be smaller (they grow on the card and are cut with `…`), so no card at Mid is an empty box. Mid starts at 70 %. A folder's name and count at Mid and In have a backing, so no dot of the ground shows through them.
- A rule waiting is a lime ring, never a filled dot, so it does not read as the fixed mark's green. Folders sort as a person counts (`Folder 2` before `Folder 10`).
- At Mid and In a note read in every session that starts a column sits as low as the first cards in a folder's frame, so card tops line up across columns.
- Closer in (In), a card's summary, rules and evidence wrap to three lines each, with 16 points below the last as above the first; Hippo.app wraps them to the card's inner width as the font sets them, on no more lines than hippo made room for.
- Pills hug their words; in dark a plain pill sits one step above the card's ground.

### The overview fills the view

hippo's layout depends on the view. `view_arrangement(w, h)` lays hippo's layout out (the notes read in every session in a first column of their own, then each folder into the lowest run of columns) in each number of columns from one to one per block, and keeps the one that shows the whole map largest: the one whose Mid fits at 70 % or more, if any, else the one whose Out fits at the largest zoom. Out cards are 72 points apart both ways, so channels between rows are as wide as gaps between columns. Hippo.app does this when Brain opens and on Fit (⌘0); `hippo view map --view 1220x690` prints it. A person's arrangement is never changed by it.

Fit shows the whole map, centred: at Mid when it fits there at 70 % or more (at most 100 %), else at Out at up to 69 %. That zoom is also the smallest: further out the map would only shrink into the middle of an empty view. (A map too big to read at Out even fitted is drawn with names only, per the 11 px rule.) The + and − buttons, and ⌘= and ⌘−, step through one table, `STYLE.steps` (15, 20, 25, 33, 40, 50, 60, 70, 85, 100, 125, 200, 300 %), about the selected card when it is in view; from a fitted zoom between two steps they go to the next one, never below the fit.

### Nothing floats over the map

Brain's header is an opaque band 128 points high with a hairline edge; the map starts below it and ends in a hairline above the Ask bar and another a gutter (18 points) short of the panel, which is a docked column: the map never runs under it, and fitting, centring and clipping use the map's own right edge. The header's first row holds the controls: `Reset layout` (kept in its place, hidden, while the layout is hippo's, so nothing moves when it shows), zoom with Lucide minus and plus and tabular figures, and `Treemap` with Lucide's layout grid. Its second row holds the headline and, right-aligned in a slot of its own, the status: `Runbooks moved. Undo` after a move, or what the line under the pointer stands for. The lead below keeps its whole width. At Mid and In, folders whose name band has scrolled above the view keep their name and count in an opaque strip at the map's top; the cards pass under it.

### Dragging a block

- The lifted block is the real block under the pointer at the point it was grabbed, 3 % larger, with a shadow; in dark it takes a lighter ground and a 1 px ring, so it reads as lifted. A folder's frame gets a solid ground.
- The lifted block is drawn 4 % larger. Where it will land is a dashed slot with a lime tint (12 % and a 75 % lime edge in dark, 8 % in light); it appears where the block lands and fades in there as the other blocks slide (150 ms, ease-out) to open it and close the old one.
- While a block is lifted, the focus's lines stay, faint (35 %), with their heads, where they will run after the drop, and stay faint while the dropped block settles.
- On release the block settles (180 ms) and `Runbooks moved. Undo` shows in the status slot for 6 seconds. A drop never rescales: the zoom stays, so cards keep their size under the pointer, and the view pans only as far as keeps the dropped block's head (its name band, or its first card) in it, below the strip of folder names at Mid and In. The smallest zoom becomes the new map's fit at once, so zooming out (or ⌘0) always reaches the whole map. Only the zoom control and Reset refit.
- On Escape the lines go back at once to where they run now, faint, while the blocks fly home. On Escape every block flies back (220 ms). ⌥-arrows, Reset and Undo slide too.
- With macOS Reduce Motion on (or `prefers-reduced-motion` in the browser) nothing slides.

### The web demo is the same map

The live demo lays the map out for the view Hippo.app would have in a window as large as the page (the replica's note sits in its top bar), so it gets the same columns and the same fit zoom. A Mid card sets its title, summary and chips where the app does (16, 50 and 74 points from its top), and the panel uses the app's type sizes; its foot fades out in both. It reads line width, corners, arrowheads, badges, zoom floors and the zoom table from the map's JSON (`style`), routes and counts with the same `lines_at`, and its colours are `brand/tokens.json`, as the app's are: `docs/site/check-tokens.mjs` fails when either drifts. Same chrome (workspace chevron, search, Agents and Guard with their counts, the composer's words and runner chip, `waiting for you`), same Lucide icons, same wordmark (`brand/hippo-mark.svg`), tabular figures in the system font, and the same hover rule.

## How it works

- `hippo_view::night::map`: `Layout.focus` holds each linked card's lines (there is no `lines` field: nothing is drawn at rest) and `Layout.badges` its badges (`Badge`: centre, links, trunk, room, the cards it leads to); `Line.trunk` groups a trunk's branches, `Line.out` and `Line.back` say which way its links run; `STYLE` (`NotesMap.style` in the JSON) is how lines and badges are drawn and the zoom table, with `Style::step`, `badge_half` and `badge_fits`; `Layout.hovered` and `hovered_badges` (the same, routed as hovered), `Layout::shown(focus)`, `badges(focus)`, `set(focus, hovered)`, `lines_at(focus, hovered, x, y, tol, z)`, `links_of(lines)`, `visible(focus)`, `Style::badge_at(badge, z)`, `ends(lines, hot)` (each line's head and which light up), `shows(line, view)`, `exits(lines, view, inset, near)` (the edge chips) and `beside(selection, hovered, card)`. `route` routes the trunks as above. `Mark.row` marks a folder card's note rows. `default_arrangement_in(cols)`, `view_arrangement(w, h)`, `NotesMap::fitted`, `NotesMap::fit`, `NotesMap::preview(arr, level)` and `block_rect_in`. `in_text` and `in_height` wrap In cards.
- Hippo.app: `MapView` gains `hover`, `hover_line`, `slide` (the layout blocks slide to, each block's offset when it started, the dropped block's scale, the drop it previews), `view_size` and `fit_zoom`. The painter draws each block at its pose and asks for another frame while a slide runs. `reduce_motion()` reads `NSWorkspace.accessibilityDisplayShouldReduceMotion`. Dev-only `HIPPO_APP_DEV_HOVER=x,y` (or `line`), `HIPPO_APP_DEV_PROGRESS`, `HIPPO_APP_DEV_SLOW`, `HIPPO_APP_DEV_REDUCE_MOTION` and `HIPPO_APP_DEV_DROP=0` set states for screenshots.
- The demo: `demo/arrange.js` ports `links`, `route` (with its badges), `lines_at`, `links_of`, `Style::step`, `view_arrangement`, `fit` and `preview`; `demo/demo.js` draws each block in a box of its own that slides with a CSS transition. `docs/site/demo-data.sh` writes the map hippo laid out for an 860 × 600 view, and `check-demo-render.mjs` fails if the port chooses other columns or lays it out differently.

## Tests

On maps of 3 to 300 notes, each in its default layout, two random arrangements and fitted to two views, at every level:

- no line enters a card or a folder's name or count, for every focus;
- no level run of a trunk comes within the clearance of a card or name band;
- no line shows at rest, and at most two trunks for any focus;
- every Out card sets its words at one size per kind, never cuts a count, and a fitted overview sets nothing under 11 px;
- every line turns at right angles, its vertical runs in gaps; two trunks never share a lane or a port, and a card has at most one port per side;
- a card with many links leaves as one trunk each way, and lines to one column share every point up to their branch;
- the notes read in every session keep the first column; on the 300-note map folder cards are one height;
- In cards wrap their words and keep 16 points below them;
- the 300-note overview fills more than 60 % of the view both ways; maps of up to 40 notes open whole at 42 % or more;
- a person's arrangement is kept when the map is fitted;
- geometry, for both routings at every zoom of the table: one badge per trunk of links out, on the root every line of that trunk (and no other) runs along, clear of every card and name band by its half-width plus 4 px; the badge and the root beside it give the trunk's count through `lines_at`; a pinned note's badges count the same at Out, Mid and In; each line carries links one way and has one head, where they arrive, and no two heads share a card edge; no line in touches a badge; with the pointer on a line only the heads of the lines under it light up; the selection's lines drawn while another card is hovered (`beside`) never run along the hovered card's; every line ends level on its card's edge with room for its head; lines into the cards either side of a gutter never meet head to head; the zoom table rises from the smallest zoom to the largest and steps as the app and demo step;
- on the 300-note map and the demo home, at every zoom of the table, in views centred on cards: every line that leaves the view ends in an edge chip on its border, and every badge drawn has a line through it;
- folders sort as a person counts; `START HERE` takes one line; a big overview keeps every count in its place.

`check-demo-render.mjs` checks the demo's port against hippo's layout (badges included), its zoom steps against Hippo.app's, and its badges against its own `linesAt` at every zoom. The app's tests cover the drag (the slot opens at the drop's column, Escape slides every block back, a drop never changes the zoom, fitted or not, and lowers the zoom floor to the new map's fit), hover focus, ⌘= stepping through the shared table, and ⌘0.

## Not in this

Curved (non-orthogonal) lines; lines between notes in one folder at Out (they are inside one card); a count on hover at Mid and In.
