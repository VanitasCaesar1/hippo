One brain for all your coding agents.

hippo finds every Claude Code and Codex session on your Mac and shows your day: what ran, what needs you, what went wrong.

macOS, Apple silicon or Intel. No sudo. Open source, MIT. Claude Code plugin and Linux

$ hippo

Your day, read from your agents' history.

Sessions, sub-agents, hours of agent work, how long agents waited on you, and each time you corrected one.

Fleet

What every agent is doing right now.

Which one needs you, and which account still has usage left.

Incidents

What went wrong, in your own words.

Each incident quotes you, and names the agent and the step.

Ask

Has an agent already solved this?

Ranks your past sessions and notes against the question.

$hippo ask "<question>"

X-ray

Does "tests pass" hold?

Shows what context a session was given, what it searched for and used, and whether its claims hold.

$hippo xray <session>

Memory

How it stays honest

Every night, hippo checks your notes against what agents actually did.

A second reviewer

Receipts
