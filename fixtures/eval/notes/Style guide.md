# Style guide

Naming and writing conventions for Tern's code, commit messages and notes.

## Code

- The formatter decides layout; never argue with it in review.
- Name things for what they are: `asset`, `album`, `share_link`, never `item` or `thing`.
- Error messages say what happened and what to do next.

## Commits

- Start with a short area word, then a lowercase sentence: `share: allow per-link expiry`.
- One logical change per commit.

## Notes

- First line under the title says what the note is in one sentence.
- Keep facts short and dated when they may change.
